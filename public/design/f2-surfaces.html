Surface, more explicit

The same 18 km route drawn several ways (shapes and surfaces drawn by hand, not real routing). Today surface is a colour in the summary, black dashes under the profile and dashes on the map line: three languages for one thing. Options 1, 2 and 6 together are the proposal; 3 and 4 are for comparison.

Now

Summary in colour, profile and map in dashes. On a small map scale the dashes merge into a line.

1 · One colour per surface, everywhere

The profile strip takes the summary's colours, in a row of its own under the grade fill. Ochre is gravel in every place.

2 · Colour the line by surface

A visible switch next to the profile: Grade / Surface. In Surface the line uses the same colours; no dashes needed.

6 · The summary in words

How much, in how many pieces, the longest; cobbles named apart. Answers "what will I ride on".

9.9 km asphalt, then mostly off it: 3.2 km gravel in 2 stretches (longest 2.1 km), 2.7 km compacted, 1.3 km dirt, 0.9 km trail. 300 m cobbles in Sintra.

6 % has no surface tag in OpenStreetMap; it is guessed from the road type.

3 · Casing: grade inside, surface on the edge

Both at once, no switch. Thin on a phone, and the line gets wide enough to hide the map under it.

4 · Only what needs attention (Road)

For a road bike everything that is not asphalt is marked on the line and in the profile; the rest stays neutral. Gravel would mark sand, mud and trails; MTB, asphalt.

off asphalt 5 stretches, 8.1 km; longest 2.1 km gravel at km 7

7a · Two-colour line: grade thick, surface thin on top

Grade 9 px, surface 3 px in the middle. Both read at once, no switch; asphalt shows as a dark core.

7b · Same, with a light gap between

A 1 px light seam around the core keeps dark grades (red, black) and dark surfaces (asphalt, dirt) apart.

7c · Asphalt without a core

Asphalt, the usual case, draws no core: only what is off asphalt gets the thin line, so it stands out.

7 · At a small scale (zoomed out, phone)

7a and 7c with the whole line at 5 px instead of 9: the core shrinks to 2 px.
