# Load test, 13 Nov 2026

Script: `tools/loadtest/loadtest.py`, run on the VPS in the maps-api network (`docker run --rm --network proxy-maps …`), so many clients can be simulated by X-Forwarded-For (maps-api lets one client compute 3 legs at once). External services (Mapillary, Panoramax, Overpass, Nominatim) were not loaded: only a place name already in our cache was asked. Server: 6 vCPU, 11 GB, shared with other services; load average 0.4 before, 3.2 at the peak.

Peaks: BRouter 5.9 cores and 809 MB of its 2 GB; maps-api 1.5 cores and 74 MB; SQLite transaction p95 20 ms.

## Findings

- **The router is the bottleneck, as expected:** about 3–3.5 cold legs per second whatever the number of clients; above 6 clients legs queue and wait (p50 grows from 1.6 s to 6 s at 24 clients). No 429 or 503: the queue absorbs it.
- **BRouter killed legs under load.** At 12 and 24 clients its watchdog cancelled 18 legs ("contention! ms killed"), returned as 400, which the app retries. Cause: maps-api sends at most 6 legs and BRouter had exactly 6 threads; a slot is freed a moment before BRouter frees its thread. **Fix:** BRouter `MAX_THREADS` 8 with maps-api slots still 6. Rerun at 12 and 24 clients: **0 kills**; the remaining 400s are "no track" for random points in water.
- **One client's long route** waits in its own line of 3: 30 legs at once took 15–18 s each, other clients are not held up.
- **Caches carry the rest:** a cached leg 376/s, a short link 529/s, a cached place name stopped only by the per-client limit (by design, it protects Nominatim).
- The test adds about 30 test addresses to "visitors today" on the business dashboard.
